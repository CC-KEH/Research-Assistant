Capabilities

Everything you need to research smarter

Built for students, academics, and professionals who need to move fast without missing what matters.

[ 01 ] — INGEST
Instant Paper Summaries

Upload any PDF or paste a DOI and receive a structured summary in seconds — key findings, methodology, and limitations clearly delineated.

[ 02 ] — QUERY
Chat With Your Sources

Ask questions directly about your documents in plain language. Every answer is cited back to the exact passage it came from.

[ 03 ] — DISCOVER
Semantic Search

Find the exact section across your entire library using meaning-based search — not just keyword matching. Thought leads to thought.

[ 04 ] — ORGANISE
Knowledge Library

All your papers, annotations, and findings in one quiet, organised workspace. The kind of order a scholar's desk rarely achieves.

[ 05 ] — WRITE
Writing Assistance

Draft literature reviews, abstracts, and citations grounded in your actual sources. Nothing invented; everything attributable.

[ 06 ] — CONNECT
Cross-Paper Insights

Surface connections, contradictions, and recurring themes across your entire collection. The synthesis you've been trying to write.

Process

From paper to insight in three steps

No complex setup, no steep learning curve. Upload and begin.

01.
Upload your papers

Drop in any PDF, paste a DOI, or connect to arXiv and PubMed. Research Assistant ingests and indexes everything automatically, ready for interrogation.

02.
Ask your questions

Converse with your entire research library in plain English. Ask for summaries, comparisons, specific data points, or thematic overviews spanning dozens of papers at once.

03.
Export and build

Export annotated summaries, citation-ready quotes, and draft sections. Everything you need to write, already organised and attributed.
